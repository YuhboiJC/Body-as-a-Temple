The Biblio Diet · Dr. Josh Axe & Jordan Rubin
"God designed food with purpose. The same foods that sustained biblical figures for centuries are exactly what modern nutritional science says we need to heal our metabolism, lose weight naturally, and feel truly alive."
— The Bibliotarian Way
The 7 Pillars of the Bibliotarian Lifestyle
Pillar 1 — Eat clean, whole foods as God created them
Foundation
Rubin and Axe teach that God repeatedly distinguished between clean and unclean foods in Scripture. The core principle: eat foods as close to their created form as possible — grass-fed meats, organic produce, properly prepared grains, raw dairy, cold-pressed oils. The more processing between seed and plate, the further from God's design. Every ingredient should be identifiable as something from the earth. If you can't pronounce it or it didn't exist 2,000 years ago, reconsider it.
"And God said, 'I give you every seed-bearing plant... and every tree that has fruit with seed in it. They will be yours for food.'" — Genesis 1:29
Pillar 2 — Fasting as spiritual and physiological discipline
Your 4:20 IMF
The Biblio Diet frames intermittent fasting as both a biblical rhythm and a metabolic tool. Moses fasted 40 days. Elijah, David, Daniel, and Jesus himself all fasted. Modern science confirms: a 20-hour fast drops insulin, triggers autophagy (cellular cleanup), upregulates fat burning, and resets metabolic function. Your 4-hour window from 1–5 PM is your daily act of stewardship. The fast is not deprivation — it is discipline and devotion in one.
"When you fast, do not look somber as the hypocrites do... but put oil on your head and wash your face." — Matthew 6:17
Pillar 3 — Biblical proteins: grass-fed meats, clean fish, eggs
Muscle + Healing
Rubin notes that the Bible's longest-lived heroes ate red meat whenever they could. Grass-fed beef, lamb, bison, and poultry are foundational. Wild-caught fish (salmon, sardines, tuna) provide omega-3s for anti-inflammation and brain health. Whole eggs are one of the most nutrient-dense foods on earth — the yolk is not the enemy. Grass-fed whey isolate is the modern equivalent of clean animal protein. Avoid factory-farmed, hormone-injected, antibiotic-laden meats — not what God designed.
"Bring the fattened calf and kill it. Let us eat and celebrate." — Luke 15:23
Pillar 4 — Olive oil, raw honey, and biblical fats
Anti-Inflammation
Extra virgin olive oil is Scripture's primary fat — mentioned over 140 times. Rich in oleocanthal, a natural anti-inflammatory compound comparable in effect to ibuprofen. Raw, local honey is the Biblio Diet's only approved sweetener — containing enzymes, antioxidants, and antimicrobial properties. Avocado and tree nuts (almonds, walnuts) align perfectly with this pillar. Avoid all industrial seed oils (canola, soybean, vegetable, corn oil) — highly processed, omega-6 dominant, and inflammatory.
"A land flowing with milk and honey." — Exodus 3:8
Pillar 5 — Daniel's vegetables and the plant kingdom
Gut Health + Longevity
Daniel 1 is the Biblio Diet's most direct food experiment: Daniel and his companions ate only vegetables and water for 10 days — and were visibly healthier and stronger than those eating the king's rich food. Modern science confirms: cruciferous vegetables, leafy greens, garlic, and onions are among the most powerful anti-cancer, anti-inflammatory foods known. Biblical fruits — pomegranate, figs, dates, grapes — are dense in antioxidants. Load your plate with plants at every meal.
"Please test your servants for ten days: Give us nothing but vegetables to eat and water to drink." — Daniel 1:12
Pillar 6 — Properly prepared grains and fermented foods
Gut Microbiome
Rubin: "With Jesus being called the bread of life, it wouldn't make sense if bread was bad for everyone. The way it's processed and preserved matters." The Biblio Diet endorses whole grain sourdough (long-fermented, which breaks down anti-nutrients and gluten proteins), barley, lentils, and chickpeas. These feed your gut microbiome — which modern science now links to immunity, mood, and metabolism. Commercial white bread made with instant yeast and 30 additives bears no resemblance to Scripture's grain.
"Jesus said to them, 'I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger.'" — John 6:35
Pillar 7 — Faith, prayer, and gratitude as healing forces
Whole Person
Rubin and Axe insist the Bibliotarian lifestyle is not purely physical. "I listened to my great physician, not my ordinary physician," Axe said of his own spinal recovery. Faith itself plays a role in healing that no macronutrient can replicate. Pray before meals — not as ritual, but as genuine gratitude for provision. Cultivate peace: chronic stress raises cortisol which sabotages every physical goal you have. Rest on the Sabbath. Honor sleep — 80% of human growth hormone is released during deep sleep. God is restoring the whole person.
"Dear friend, I pray that you may enjoy good health and that all may go well with you, even as your soul is getting along well." — 3 John 1:2
